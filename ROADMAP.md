# Roadmap I0 → I14

## FR

Le masterbook est organisé en 15 étapes pédagogiques, I0 incluse. Chaque itération doit produire :
1. notions fondamentales ;
2. traduction architecturale ;
3. artefacts ;
4. anti-patterns ;
5. questions d'entretien ;
6. lab MayaBank lorsque pertinent.

## EN

The masterbook is organised into 15 learning stages including I0. Every iteration must provide:
1. fundamentals;
2. architecture interpretation;
3. deliverables;
4. anti-patterns;
5. interview questions;
6. a MayaBank lab when relevant.

## Definition of Done

Une itération est complète si / An iteration is complete when:
- concepts are bilingual;
- official terminology is preserved;
- legal facts point to official sources;
- architecture decisions are separated from legal requirements;
- one concrete banking/insurance example exists;
- outputs can be reused in a client mission;
- limitations and assumptions are explicit.
