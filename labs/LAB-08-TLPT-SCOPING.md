# Lab 08 — TLPT Scoping

## Scenario
MayaBank is preparing a threat-led test of its instant payment capability.

## Tasks
Identify:
- critical live systems;
- external interfaces;
- IAM/PKI;
- fraud/core/clearing;
- admin channels;
- monitoring/SOC;
- relevant providers;
- safe boundaries;
- excluded systems with rationale;
- test-data/safety constraints;
- recovery contacts.

## Discuss
Why should the architect understand TLPT without becoming the red-team operator?
