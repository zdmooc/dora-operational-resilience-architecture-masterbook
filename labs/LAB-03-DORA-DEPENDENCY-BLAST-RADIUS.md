# Lab 03 — Dependency & Blast Radius

## Scenario
IAM platform is unavailable.

## Tasks
1. List direct consumers.
2. Traverse transitive dependencies.
3. Separate new authentication, existing JWT validation, service-to-service identity and privileged operator access.
4. Map business impact.
5. Identify safe degraded modes.
6. Define evidence needed during a test.

## Bonus
Repeat for DNS and Kafka/MQ.
